# 📊 Financial Performance & Revenue Analysis

## 🎯 Project Objective
The goal of this project was to analyze organizational financial health, identify growth trends in revenue, and find areas where costs could be optimized to improve the overall "Ratio" of liquidity.

---

#NOTE: "FTP" refers to "FOR THAT PERIOD"
      "TTD"refers to "TOTAL TO DATE"

## 🔍 Key solutions  & Insights :

*   **Revenue Growth:** Identified a **year-over-year increase** in secondary service income.
*   **Cost Savings:** Discovered a **redundancy** in administrative overhead costs through ledger mapping.
*   **Data integrity & Efficiency:** Successfully implemented a "Linguistic Schema" to standardize financial reporting across disparate departments, reducing manual reconciliation time by
*   *automated generation of complete financial statements
*   *real time data refresh eliminates manual data entry and reconciliation
*   *single source of truth for all financial data reduces discrepancies or conflicts . 
*   **Growth correlation:** There is a direct positive correlation between "Investment" allocations and "Ratio" improvements in long-term asset liquidity.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used
*   **Data source:** excel & xml files
*   **Power BI Desktop:** power query, Data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive visualization.
*   **Data Transformation:** Cleaning raw ledger data and categorizing revenue pillars.
*   **Financial Analysis:** Trend analysis, budget-to-actual comparisons, and ratio tracking.
